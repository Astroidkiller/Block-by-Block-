Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about starting with Block by Block.

Do our structural engineers or consultants need to pay for an account?

No! External consultants (architects, MEP consultants, structural engineers, municipal liaisons) are 100% free. You can invite unlimited external team members via their phone number or email, and they can review and sign off without paying a rupee.

How does the mobile WhatsApp / phone invite workflow work?

When you assign a clearance check or request a sign-off, Block by Block generates a secure, one-tap mobile link sent straight via WhatsApp or SMS. Consultants don't even need to download an app; they review the drawing and tap 'Approve & Sign' directly from their browser.

Can I upgrade or downgrade as project phases finish?

Yes. All subscriptions are flexible. You can scale from Starter to Growth as you launch more towers, or downgrade between project delivery cycles with zero penalty.

Are the Proceed / Hold reports admissible for RERA and lender compliance?

Yes. Every phase transition creates an immutable, timestamped PDF report showing who uploaded the drawings, which engineer cleared the calculations, and the exact sign-off log for investor and municipal compliance.
