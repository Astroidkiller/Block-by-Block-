The 5 Gated Construction Stages

No contractor advances to the next stage without every mandatory clearance signed.

STAGE 01✓ MANDATORY GATE

Pre-Construction & Land Survey

Critical Clearance Checks:

  • ✓Geotechnical Soil Bearing Report
  • ✓Total Station Boundary NOC
  • ✓RERA Project Filing
  • ✓Excavation Depth Profile

Required Sign-offs:

Lead Geotechnical Consultant · Surveyor · VP Development

⚠ Overrun Risk Prevented: Prevents foundation settlement, boundary disputes, and municipal stop-work notices.

STAGE 02✓ MANDATORY GATE

Substructure & Foundation Pour

Critical Clearance Checks:

  • ✓Column Reinforcement DWG-S-104 rev D
  • ✓RMC Mix Design 40 Grade
  • ✓Water Table Dewatering Clearance
  • ✓Bar Bending Schedule (BBS)

Required Sign-offs:

Lead Structural Consultant · Site Project Manager

⚠ Overrun Risk Prevented: Prevents defective column cold joints and premature concrete pours before peer verification.

STAGE 03✓ MANDATORY GATE

Superstructure & Structural Framing

Critical Clearance Checks:

  • ✓PT Slab Tendon Layout
  • ✓Shear Wall Shuttering Verticality
  • ✓Concrete Cube 7-Day & 28-Day Strength Tests
  • ✓Seismic Connection Nodes

Required Sign-offs:

Structural Peer Reviewer · Third-Party QA Lab

⚠ Overrun Risk Prevented: Avoids costly slab retrofits, column jacketing, and structural deflection disputes.

STAGE 04✓ MANDATORY GATE

MEP Integration & Fire Clearance

Critical Clearance Checks:

  • ✓3D BIM Clash Detection Report
  • ✓Plumbing Core Drill Clearances
  • ✓Municipal Fire NOC Verification
  • ✓Electrical Transformer Substation Clear

Required Sign-offs:

MEP Consultant · Fire Safety Liaison

⚠ Overrun Risk Prevented: Prevents breaking newly cast concrete to route overlooked piping and ductwork.

STAGE 05✓ MANDATORY GATE

Finishing, Snag List & Handover

Critical Clearance Checks:

  • ✓Waterproofing Pressure Ponding Tests
  • ✓Facade Wind Load Certification
  • ✓Occupancy Certificate (OC) Inspection
  • ✓Zero Open High-Priority Snags

Required Sign-offs:

Chief Architect · Handover Quality Director

⚠ Overrun Risk Prevented: Eliminates buyer handover disputes, customer warranty claims, and delayed final loan disbursements.
